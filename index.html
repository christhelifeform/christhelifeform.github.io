Channel Tools

Command-line utilities Chris Marriott uses on his own YouTube channels.

Channel Tools is a small set of scripts that run on the owner's own computer. They read analytics for channels the owner manages and, on request, update the title, description and tags of the owner's own videos through Google's official YouTube APIs.

The tools are used by one person, on their own channels. They are not a public service, have no sign-up, and are not offered to anyone else.
