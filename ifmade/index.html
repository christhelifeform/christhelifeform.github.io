ifmade

An engine Chris Marriott runs to make and publish short videos on his own YouTube channels.

ifmade runs on the owner's own computer. It writes and renders short videos, publishes them to channels the owner manages through Google's official YouTube APIs, and reads those channels' analytics to learn what works.

It is used by one person, on their own channels. It is not a public service, has no sign-up, and is not offered to anyone else.
