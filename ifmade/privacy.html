Privacy Policy

ifmade · effective October 23, 2026

ifmade is an engine operated by Chris Marriott that makes short videos and publishes them to YouTube channels he owns or manages. This page describes what it accesses and what happens to that data.

What the engine accesses

Through Google's YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, the engine uploads videos to the connected channels and sets their titles, descriptions, tags, captions, thumbnails and playlists; posts and pins comments from the channel's own account; reads the connected channel's identity to confirm it is the right channel; and reads views, watch time and related analytics for the connected channels.

Whose data

Only the operator's own channels are connected. The engine is used by one person and is not offered to the public. No one else's Google account is ever connected to it.

Where data is stored

Authorization tokens are kept in the keychain of the operator's own computer. Analytics the engine downloads are kept in the operator's private files and private code repository, and the videos it makes in the operator's own cloud storage.

Short links

Videos may show short links on ifmade.link. When a viewer opens one, the operator's link service records the time, the link, the country, the device type and the name of the referring site, then forwards the viewer. It does not record IP addresses, browser details or anything that identifies a person.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone. The engine has no advertising partners.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The engine's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party access page, or by deleting the token from the operator's keychain.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will appear on this page with a new effective date.
